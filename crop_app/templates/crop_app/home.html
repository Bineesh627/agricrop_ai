About AgriCrop AI

AgriCrop AI assists farmers in selecting the most suitable crops based on soil characteristics and environmental conditions. The application allows users to input agricultural parameters such as NPK nutrients, soil pH, temperature, humidity, and rainfall. Using Machine Learning algorithms trained on historical agricultural datasets, the system provides accurate recommendations and cultivation guidance to maximize agricultural productivity.
